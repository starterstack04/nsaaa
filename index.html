Welcome to EHR NEXUS

At the end of the activity, participants will be able to: Demonstrate basic skills in using an Electronic Health Record. Accurately document and retrieve patient information. Apply nursing informatics concepts in a simulated clinical setting. Identify relevant patient data and abnormal findings. Promote patient safety through accurate electronic documentation. Demonstrate awareness of privacy, confidentiality, and data security. Develop teamwork, critical thinking, and decision-making skills.

ABOUT US

Participants will act as student nurses using a simulated Electronic Health Record (EHR) to assess, document, retrieve, and interpret patient information. They must complete assigned EHR tasks accurately while demonstrating proper documentation, data privacy, patient safety, and effective use of health information technology.

Each team can sign in using their assigned account.
